5 · Not Supported (download-only)

  • AV1 / VP9 in MP4 or MKV on machines whose WebView2 lacks the decoder — user gets an explicit "download and play externally" message.
  • AVI, WMV, FLV, MPEG-2 video — no pipeline; native fallback will fail on stock WebView2 (mpegts.js explicitly does not support mpeg2video).
  • Embedded subtitle tracks — never extracted (any tier); only sidecar files.
  • Multiple audio tracks — first/primary track only in every tier (no track selector).

6 · Fallback Chains (safety nets)

  • MP4: MSE unsupported codec → HEVC-family? /remux : native probe → else "unsupported" message.
  • MKV H.264: transmuxer init failure → /remux → mpegts.js.
  • TS: mpegts.js fatal (LATM bugs, decode errors, unsupported codec) → /remux (native or mpegts.js re-attach).
  • Unknown container: straight to native <video>.
